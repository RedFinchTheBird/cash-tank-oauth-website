About Cash Tank Budget

Cash Tank Budget is a personal budgeting application that runs on the user's own Windows computer. It helps individuals track spending, categorize transactions, and manage budgets. Users may optionally connect their financial accounts so the application can import transactions and balances automatically. Account connections are provided securely through Plaid.

What Data We Access and Why

With the user's explicit permission, Cash Tank Budget uses Plaid to connect the user's bank, credit card, and Venmo accounts to import account balances and transaction history. This data is used solely to provide budgeting and cash‑flow features to the user within the application. We do not sell, rent, or share the data for advertising. Transaction data is stored on the user's own device.

  • Bank credentials are entered directly with Plaid; Cash Tank Budget never sees or stores a user's online‑banking username or password.
  • Cash Tank Budget requests only the transactions data needed for budgeting. It does not use money‑movement, identity, or credit‑underwriting products.

How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption in transit: all communication uses HTTPS/TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Encryption at rest: Plaid access keys are stored in Microsoft Azure Key Vault; connection metadata is stored in encrypted Azure storage.
  • No server‑side transaction storage: transactions pass through to the user's device and are not retained on our servers.
  • Least‑privilege access and multi‑factor authentication protect administrative access to our systems.
  • The application's license credential is stored encrypted on the user's device.

Cash Tank Budget Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Cash Tank Budget ("the App") is a personal budgeting application provided by an independent individual developer ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the App and choose to connect a financial account.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: a license key and related activation details.

Collected automatically for licensing: a device identifier generated on your computer and activation status, used to validate your license and prevent misuse.

Financial information via Plaid: if you link an account, Plaid securely collects your login credentials on Plaid's systems and provides us with account information (name, type, mask, balance) and transaction history. We never see or store your bank username or password.

3. How we use information

To activate and validate your license, connect your accounts, import transactions and balances, provide and improve budgeting features, and respond to your requests. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Where your data is stored

Your imported transactions, account details, and budgets are stored locally on your own computer. To keep connections working, we store an encrypted Plaid access key (in Azure Key Vault) and limited connection metadata (in encrypted Azure storage). We do not store your transaction history on our servers.

5. How we share information

  • Plaid — to connect your accounts and retrieve transactions. See Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Azure — our cloud provider, which stores encrypted credentials and metadata on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your Plaid access key and connection metadata only while your account remains linked. When you unlink an institution in the App, we automatically disconnect it from Plaid, delete the stored access key, and delete the connection metadata. You can remove locally stored data by deleting it in the App or uninstalling the App. To request deletion of any personal information we hold, contact us (Section 10); we respond within 30 days as required by applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

You control whether to connect any account and can disconnect at any time in the App. Depending on where you live (for example, California and certain other U.S. states), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's privacy

Cash Tank Budget is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and posting the revised policy on this page.
